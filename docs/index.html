04 / ACCESS

Get Vigil on
your computer.

Free, no account. On Mac it installs like any app — drag it into Applications and open it. The first launch sets up its AI components once (~2 GB); every launch after that is instant.

DOWNLOAD FOR MAC DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS

Mac — drag & drop

Open Vigil.dmg and drag Vigil into Applications. Double-click to open. First time only: right-click → Open → Open. Your browser opens to Vigil — create the admin account and you're watching.

Windows — one‑click

Run Vigil-Setup.exe and follow the prompts — it installs like any app and adds a Start Menu shortcut. If SmartScreen warns, choose More info → Run anyway (the app is just new, not unsafe). No unzipping, no Python.

Linux

Unzip and run ./Vigil-Linux.sh — it sets itself up on the first run and opens the dashboard when ready.

05 / FOR INVIGILATORS

The app in your pocket,
on exam day.

If your school already runs Vigil, put the invigilator app on your phone. Get an alert the instant a phone is spotted in your room, and open any camera — on demand. No app store, no configuration; it works over your school's Wi‑Fi.

GET THE APP — 3 STEPS
1
Open your school linkYour Vigil admin sends you a link (or a QR). Enter it on the right, or scan the QR — you and the Vigil must be on the same Wi‑Fi.
2
Add to Home ScreeniPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap Install. Vigil becomes a real app icon.
3
Sign in & watchLog in with the account your admin created — you'll see your cameras and get alerts while Vigil is open.
OPEN YOUR SCHOOL'S VIGIL

Paste the link from your school's Vigil (Settings → Watch on your phone). You and the Vigil must be on the same Wi‑Fi.

Why a link and not an app-store download? Vigil runs inside your school and streams only to its own people. Installing from your school's own address is what keeps footage private and your alerts instant.
